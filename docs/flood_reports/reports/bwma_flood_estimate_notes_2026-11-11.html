Method and comparability

The November 2026 entry is the official cumulative inventory (Sentinel-2 open water plus radar flooded vegetation, accepted scene by scene from the corrected Oct 24 base), not a monthly Sentinel-2 NIR 15-day median composite. It is not comparable with the earlier monthly bars on the dashboard. The Estimate is the upper end of the inventory (499.60 ac). Earlier monthly composites (Aug 2024 to Oct 2026) also carry the composite pixel-area rule, which overstated area by about 25%; those values have not been restated on the dashboard.

Sentinel-2 reflectance conversion check

Since January 2022 (ESA processing baseline 04.00), Sentinel-2 surface reflectance carries a −1000 digital-number offset. Our scripts have always used Earth Engine’s harmonized collection (COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED), which removes that offset, and divide band 8 by 10,000; the repository history shows no change in collection or scaling since the first version (June 2024).

In a November 2026 check of 13 report windows from 2024 to 2026, every scene carried the offset. The harmonized data read 0.00–0.01 over the reference lakes in 12 of 13 windows (0.17 in the May 20, 2025 window) and 0.23–0.38 over the East Winterton dry control, including the windows of the 0.08–0.10 summer reports. The older non-harmonized collection (COPERNICUS/S2_SR) reads 0.10 higher in every window. We found no conversion shift in the data behind the low summer cutpoints; an uncorrected offset would raise NIR readings, not lower them.
