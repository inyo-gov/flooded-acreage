## Fall NIR threshold dive (2026-09-30 afternoon)

**Question:** Sep 29 latest-usable-scene at 0.14 (106.6 ac) sits **below** Sep 26 wet-up composite at 0.14 (115.7 ac). Does fall imagery need a slightly higher NIR threshold so dark fringe wet pixels count as water? (`flood = NIR < threshold` → higher threshold → more acres.)

### Acreage comparison

| Product | NIR threshold | Total acres | Δ vs 0.14 |
|---------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Sep 29 single scene** (20260929 T11SLA) | **0.14** | **106.6** | — |
| Sep 29 single scene | 0.15 | 119.5 | +12.9 |
| Sep 29 single scene | **0.16** | **137.5** | **+30.9** |
| Sep 29 single scene | 0.18 | 175.4 | +68.8 |
| **Sep 26 composite** (start 2026-09-25, 3 scenes, label 09-26) | **0.14** | **115.7** | — |
| Sep 26 composite | 0.15 | 131.1 | +15.4 |
| Sep 26 composite | 0.16 | 151.6 | +35.9 |

### Interpretation (for Desk / Zac)

- At the **same** threshold, the Sep 29 scene stays a bit **under** the Sep 26 composite (~9–14 ac). That gap is product/window difference (one clear pass vs 3-scene median), not only fringe.
- Raising the **scene** threshold to **0.15** (~119.5 ac) roughly matches the composite at 0.14 (~115.7). **0.16** (~137.5) clearly pulls in dark fringe and still looks plausible for early wet-up.
- **0.18** (~175 ac) jumps hard (Drew roughly doubles vs 0.16) — likely over-inclusive for calibration; kept for the table only.
- **Decision for displayed latest-scene card:** **NIR 0.16** (137.5 ac), with method notes in the collapsed `<details>`.
- **Mid-month / wet-up composites stay at 0.14** unless Desk later chooses to retune the whole fall series. Compare composites at 0.15/0.16 were quarantined under `flood_reports/threshold_dive/` so they do not pollute the dashboard archive or monthly chart.
